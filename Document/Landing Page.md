## 1. Project Overview

AUREN is a personal growth and lifestyle web application designed to
help people organize and improve different areas of their daily lives.

The product brings together four pillars:

1.  **Fitness & Wellness** --- workouts, adaptive routines, and physical
    progress.
2.  **Productivity** --- daily planning, to-do lists, habits, and
    activity history.
3.  **Learning & Personal Growth** --- learning goals, skill discovery,
    and study tracking.
4.  **Spirituality & Reflection** --- personal spiritual routines,
    worship tracking, reading records, and reflection.

The landing page introduces the product and communicates its purpose. It
should feel like the beginning of a personal journey---not a generic
productivity SaaS website.

### Product vision

> A space to move better, learn more, stay consistent, and grow into the
> person you want to become.

### Primary landing page goals

-   Establish a memorable brand identity.
-   Explain AUREN's value proposition quickly.
-   Introduce the four product pillars.
-   Show a glimpse of the future product dashboard.
-   Encourage visitors to explore or begin their journey.

### Current scope

**Build the landing page only.**

Do not build authentication, database functionality, a complete
dashboard, or working habit-tracking features in this phase. The page
may contain visual mockups of future product functionality.

## 2. Creative Direction

### Concept: Immersive 3D Lifestyle Experience

Create a premium, minimal, cinematic landing page with visual
storytelling inspired by the supplied reference.

The reference direction features: - Large centered typography. -
Immersive hero composition. - Abstract 3D objects and environments. -
Smooth transitions between sections. - Generous spacing and editorial
layouts. - Motion that supports the story rather than distracting from
it.

### AUREN-specific adaptation

Do **not** reproduce the reference's brand, exact artwork, text, or
visual assets. Translate its design language into AUREN's own identity.

Use: - Organic floating forms. - Soft glowing spheres or orbs. -
Abstract landscapes or floating platforms. - Subtle botanical and
wellness-inspired shapes. - Soft atmospheric lighting. - Depth, shadows,
and restrained parallax.

Avoid making the entire experience look like a space-themed website. The
visual world should suggest personal growth, balance, calm, intention,
and progress.

### Desired emotional response

Visitors should feel: - Calm and inspired. - Curious about the
product. - Motivated to take a small first step. - Like they are
entering a thoughtfully designed digital space.

### Design keywords

`immersive` · `cinematic` · `premium` · `minimal` · `organic` · `calm` ·
`intentional` · `editorial` · `modern`

## 4. Visual Design System

### Color palette

Use a natural, premium palette rather than the reference's original
colors.

  Token         Suggested value   Usage
  ------------- ----------------- -----------------------------------------
  Forest        `#14251B`         Dark sections, primary brand background
  Deep Forest   `#0D1711`         Hero / immersive dark backgrounds
  Sage          `#879B83`         Secondary visual accents
  Soft Lime     `#D7FF7F`         CTA, highlights, active states
  Warm Cream    `#F6F5EF`         Light sections
  Pale Green    `#E3EBDD`         Feature cards and subtle surfaces
  Charcoal      `#202720`         Text on light backgrounds
  Muted Text    `#687267`         Secondary text
  White         `#FFFFFF`         Text on dark surfaces

Color values may be adjusted during implementation to maintain contrast
and visual harmony.

### Typography

Use a modern sans-serif with a strong editorial feel.

Suggested options: - **Primary:** Geist / Geist Sans - **Alternative:**
Inter, Manrope, or another clean contemporary sans-serif - Optional
display font only if it strengthens the visual hierarchy without harming
readability.

Typography rules: - Hero headline: large, bold or semibold, tight
line-height. - Section headlines: clear and expressive. - Body text:
restrained width, comfortable line-height. - Navigation and labels:
small, crisp, and legible. - Avoid too many font families or weights.

### Layout principles

-   Strong visual hierarchy.
-   Generous whitespace.
-   Clear separation between sections.
-   Wide content containers.
-   Avoid crowded cards and unnecessary UI elements.
-   Use asymmetry intentionally.
-   Keep navigation understated.
-   Make the hero visually dominant.

### Shapes and surfaces

-   Rounded corners may be used, but avoid rounding every element
    excessively.
-   Use soft shadows only when they add depth.
-   Avoid excessive glassmorphism.
-   Avoid unnecessary gradients.
-   Use gradients only for atmospheric lighting or subtle depth.
-   Keep UI mockups believable and coherent.

## 5. Landing Page Structure

The page should be a carefully paced story from inspiration to product
understanding to action.

### Section 01 --- Navigation

**Purpose:** Establish brand identity and provide simple navigation.

Elements: - AUREN logo/wordmark. - Links: `The Experience`, `Features`,
`About` (or equivalent). - Primary CTA: `Start Your Journey`.

Behavior: - Transparent or minimal navigation over the hero. - May
become a subtle solid/blurred surface after scrolling, if it fits the
design. - Mobile navigation should be simple and accessible.

Do not overpopulate the navbar.

### Section 02 --- Hero

**Purpose:** Communicate the core promise and create a memorable first
impression.

Suggested copy:

Eyebrow: `YOUR PERSONAL GROWTH COMPANION`

Headline: \> Build a life that feels like yours.

Supporting text: \> A space to move better, learn more, stay consistent,
and grow into the person you want to become.

Primary CTA: `Start Your Journey`

Secondary CTA: `Explore AUREN`

Visual direction: - Large immersive 3D composition. - Organic floating
orb(s), abstract landscape, or sculptural forms. - Atmospheric lighting
and depth. - Visual should support the headline, not obscure it. - Keep
sufficient negative space around text.

The hero should feel premium and cinematic. Use a clear focal point and
avoid visual clutter.

### Section 03 --- Introduction / Philosophy

**Purpose:** Transition from the immersive hero into the product
philosophy.

Suggested headline: \> Small steps. A life in motion.

Suggested copy: \> Growth doesn't happen all at once. It happens in the
moments you choose to show up---for your body, your goals, your
curiosity, and what matters most.

Visual direction: - Calm transition from dark hero to a light or
mid-tone section. - Large editorial typography. - Optional subtle
scroll-linked movement. - Avoid excessive text.

### Section 04 --- Four Pillars

**Purpose:** Explain the product's core areas.

Section heading: \> Everything that makes you, you.

Supporting text: \> Four parts of your life. One place to grow.

Four feature areas:

#### Fitness & Wellness

Headline: `Move with intention.` Description:
`Build routines that fit your energy, goals, and everyday life.` Visual:
Abstract fitness-oriented object or clean workout UI preview.

#### Productivity

Headline: `Make each day count.` Description:
`Plan your day, organize tasks, and keep track of the things that matter.`
Visual: Daily planner or task list mockup.

#### Learning & Growth

Headline: `Stay curious.` Description:
`Discover new skills, set learning goals, and keep your progress visible.`
Visual: Learning roadmap or progress card.

#### Spirituality & Reflection

Headline: `Grow with purpose.` Description:
`Create space for spiritual routines, reflection, and meaningful consistency.`
Visual: Calm, respectful, understated visual treatment.

Design: - Use editorial cards or a carefully composed grid. - Cards
should not look like a generic template. - Allow visual variation while
keeping consistent spacing and typography. - Ensure spiritual content is
presented respectfully and inclusively.

### Section 05 --- Product Showcase

**Purpose:** Show how AUREN may bring the pillars together.

Suggested headline: \> Your progress, in perspective.

Suggested description: \> See your routines, goals, and progress come
together in one personal space.

Visual: - A polished dashboard mockup. - Show examples of: - Daily
overview. - Habit streaks. - Progress summary. - Activity history. -
Workout / learning progress. - Combine the UI mockup with restrained
abstract 3D elements if appropriate.

Important: - This is a **static or simulated product preview** for the
landing page. - Do not imply the dashboard features are already
functional. - Do not build real application logic in this phase.

### Section 06 --- Personal Growth / Scroll Storytelling

**Purpose:** Reinforce the idea of progress over time.

Suggested headline: \> Become, one day at a time.

Suggested copy: \> You don't need to change everything today. Start with
one meaningful action---and let it grow.

Visual direction: - A larger visual or 3D scene that evolves subtly as
the user scrolls. - Use movement to communicate gradual growth, balance,
or progression. - Avoid overly complex animations that make content hard
to read. - If scroll-linked animation is too expensive or unreliable,
use simple entrance animations and layered CSS transforms.

### Section 07 --- Final CTA

**Purpose:** Give visitors a clear next step.

Suggested headline: \> Start becoming your better self.

Supporting text: \> Make space for the habits, goals, and moments that
matter.

CTA: `Start Your Journey`

Visual: - Memorable closing composition. - Soft lime accent against
forest green. - Optional abstract orb or organic 3D form. - Keep the CTA
visually prominent.

CTA behavior for this phase: - May scroll to a relevant section, open a
demo placeholder, or use a clearly labeled placeholder link. - Do not
implement fake signup functionality. - Do not show a success message for
a form that is not connected.

### Section 08 --- Footer

Include: - AUREN wordmark. - Short brand statement. - Navigation
links. - Copyright placeholder. - Optional social links only if real
destinations are available.

Keep the footer minimal.

## 6. Motion & Interaction

Motion should feel deliberate, smooth, and premium.

### Recommended motion

-   Gentle floating movement for 3D objects.
-   Subtle fade/slide-in on section entry.
-   Small hover transitions on buttons and links.
-   Optional light parallax in the hero.
-   Subtle nav transition on scroll.
-   Smooth scrolling where appropriate.

### Motion intensity

Target: **3/5 --- moderate and tasteful.**

### Avoid

-   Constant movement on every element.
-   Aggressive scroll hijacking.
-   Long delays before content appears.
-   Excessive bouncing or spring effects.
-   Animations that prevent users from reading.
-   Animations that cause layout shifts.
-   Heavy scroll-triggered timelines on mobile.

### Accessibility

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. When reduced motion is enabled: -
Disable or simplify parallax. - Reduce floating movement. - Replace
complex transitions with minimal fades or instant state changes. - Keep
all content available.

## 7. 3D Visual Implementation

3D is an important part of the visual identity, but performance and
maintainability matter.

### Preferred approach

Evaluate these options in order:

1.  **CSS transforms / layered images** for simple floating effects.
2.  **React Three Fiber + Three.js** for a genuinely interactive or
    animated 3D hero.
3.  A pre-rendered 3D asset or video when real-time 3D is unnecessary.

Do not use a complex 3D engine for every section.

### 3D requirements

-   Hero is the main location for 3D.
-   Use a limited number of objects.
-   Optimize geometry, textures, and lighting.
-   Avoid unnecessary post-processing.
-   Ensure the scene does not block text or CTA interaction.
-   Add a fallback visual if WebGL is unavailable.
-   Use a simpler visual on smaller screens or low-performance devices.
-   Keep 3D canvas layers from capturing pointer events unless
    interaction is intentional.

### Performance fallback

If the 3D scene harms performance: - Reduce object count and effects. -
Use a static rendered image or lightweight CSS composition. - Prioritize
the readability and responsiveness of the page.

## 8. Responsive Design

The page must work well on:

-   Desktop.
-   Laptop.
-   Tablet.
-   Mobile.

### Desktop

-   Large cinematic hero.
-   Spacious typography.
-   Wide visual compositions.
-   Multi-column feature sections where appropriate.

### Tablet

-   Reduce headline size and visual complexity.
-   Rebalance 3D composition.
-   Maintain clear section spacing.
-   Avoid cramped multi-column cards.

### Mobile

-   Stack content vertically.
-   Use smaller but still expressive headlines.
-   Simplify or disable heavy 3D effects.
-   Keep CTAs easy to tap.
-   Prevent horizontal overflow.
-   Ensure text remains readable against visual backgrounds.
-   Prioritize page speed and usability.

Do not simply shrink the desktop design. Recompose the experience for
mobile.
